# Coefficient diagnostics

Generated by `src/model.py`. Convention: WoE = ln(%good / %bad), positive = safer; TARGET = 1 means payment difficulty. Every coefficient is therefore expected to be **negative**: a positive one would give a riskier bin more points.

## Investigation order for each unexpected sign

1. **WoE sign convention** — checked at run time in `src/binning.py`: the riskiest EXT_SOURCE_3 bin has negative WoE and optbinning's WoE equals ln(%good/%bad) (see `selected_features.json`).
2. **Bin definitions** — the feature is refitted alone; a negative univariate coefficient means its bins rank risk in the expected direction on their own.
3. **Correlation and VIF** on train WoE values.
4. **Target encoding** — TARGET used as delivered (1 = payment difficulty), checked in `src/clean.py`.
5. **Leakage** — every candidate's IV is checked against the leak flag (`iv_summary.csv`).

**Rule** (declared in `config/settings.yaml`, `model.sign_fix_max_gini_loss = 0.005`): a feature with a reversed sign is removed only if its univariate sign is correct (so the reversal comes from overlap with other features, i.e. multicollinearity) and removing it costs less than that much validation Gini. One feature is removed at a time, smallest Gini loss first, then the model is refitted and re-checked. Note: this rule was written after the first fit flagged reversed signs; it follows the standard practice in the project brief, and its effect on Gini is reported below.
